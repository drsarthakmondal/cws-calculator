Cricket Win Score Calculator

Load player stats, choose a model (or enter your own coefficients and series totals), and get every batter's and bowler's points. Batting and bowling points each sum to half the series total.

Recognised columns. Batters: Player, Team, Runs, Balls Faced (or Balls), Wickets Lost (or Dismissals). Bowlers: Player, Team, Runs Conceded (or Runs), Balls Bowled, Wickets Taken (or Wickets). Players with 0 balls are excluded.
