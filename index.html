Cricket Win Score Calculator

Load player stats, choose a model (or enter your own coefficients and series totals), and get every batter's and bowler's points. Batting and bowling points each sum to half the series total.

How to use this calculator

What it does

It scores every batter and bowler in a series from their runs, balls and wickets, and turns that into points and wins. Batting points add up to half of the series' total points, and bowling points add up to the other half.

Quick start

  1. Choose a model in step 1, or choose Custom to enter your own coefficients.
  2. Load your stats file in step 2 (.xlsx or .csv).
  3. In step 3, check the series list. If your model has built-in totals for your series, you can leave the boxes empty. Otherwise type each series' total points.
  4. Click Calculate, read the tables and charts, and use Download Excel to keep the results.

Preparing your data file

Use an .xlsx workbook with a Batters sheet and a Bowlers sheet, or a .csv for one table at a time (a .csv must use the heading Balls Faced for batters or Balls Bowled for bowlers so the tool can tell them apart). Column names are matched ignoring capitals and spaces.

Choosing a model

The preset models use fixed coefficients that are calculated on the server and are not shown. A model that has built-in totals for your series calculates straight away. A model without them asks you to type each series' total points. Custom lets you enter your own three coefficients for batters (runs, balls faced, wickets lost) and three for bowlers (runs conceded, balls bowled, wickets taken), and your own series totals.

Series total points

This is the total number of league points awarded in the series, added up across all teams (for example 2 points per win). It sets how many points are shared out. If a series has a built-in total, the tool uses it and any number you type for that series is ignored. If it has none, type it in step 3, or paste several at once under "Paste points in bulk" (one line per series: name, then points).

Reading the results

  • Points is each player's share of the series points. It can be negative if a player cost their team more than they added.
  • Batting points sum to half the series total, and so do bowling points.
  • Wins in the charts is Points divided by "Points per win" (2 by default). Change that box if one win is worth a different number of points in your competition.
  • Click a column heading to sort. The Excel download contains all rows and both tables.

Reading the charts

  • Batters: runs scored against wins. Bowlers: wickets taken against wins.
  • The two dark lines are the medians of the players shown. Players in the top-right quadrant are above the median on both measures.
  • Only players with at least 5% of the most balls faced (or bowled) by any single player in the same series are shown. Change the percentage in the box above the charts. Pick one series to see its exact cut-off.
  • Hover over, or tap, a dot to see the player.

Limits

Up to 6,000 rows per table and 200 series per calculation, and about 30 calculations a minute. Larger files should be split by series.

Troubleshooting

  • "Series skipped, no total points available": the model has no built-in total for that series name. Type the total in step 3, or rename your file to match the series.
  • Nothing is listed, or fewer players than expected: check the column names above. Rows with 0 balls are excluded.
  • Calculate is greyed out: enter at least one series total, or choose a model that has built-in totals.
  • The model list shows only Custom: the page could not reach the calculation server. Reload, and try again in a minute.
  • Charts look empty: lower the minimum balls percentage, or choose "All series".
